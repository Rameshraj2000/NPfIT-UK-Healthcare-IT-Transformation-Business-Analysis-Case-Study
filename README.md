# NPfIT UK Healthcare IT Transformation — Business Analysis Case Study

## Project Overview

This retrospective case study examines the UK National Programme for IT (NPfIT), a large-scale healthcare IT transformation initiative launched in 2002 to modernise digital services across the National Health Service.

The project analyses programme delivery challenges from a Business Analysis perspective and proposes improvements to requirements management, stakeholder engagement, change management, governance and performance measurement.

## Business Problem

The programme faced significant delivery challenges, including delays, cost overruns, technical difficulties and resistance from healthcare professionals. The case explores how business and operational factors can affect the implementation and adoption of enterprise IT systems.

## Objectives

- Analyse key business and operational challenges.
- Identify contributing factors and potential root causes.
- Examine stakeholder interests and engagement needs.
- Assess potential business process and change impacts.
- Recommend improvements and define proposed success measures.

## Business Analysis Activities

- Business problem definition and scope identification
- Root-cause analysis
- Stakeholder identification and analysis
- AS-IS and TO-BE process analysis
- Change impact assessment
- Recommendations and implementation roadmap
- KPI definition and benefits measurement

## Key Recommendations

1. Strengthen requirements elicitation and scope control.
2. Involve clinical and operational stakeholders throughout delivery.
3. Assess workflow impacts and provide structured training.
4. Improve governance, accountability and risk management.
5. Adopt phased implementation with appropriate testing and readiness checks.
6. Track outcomes through agreed performance indicators.

## Deliverables

- Business problem statement
- Root-cause analysis
- Stakeholder analysis
- Business process and change impact analysis
- Recommendations and implementation roadmap
- Proposed success measures

## Tools and Approach

The case study uses secondary research, structured business analysis and retrospective interpretation of an academic case.

No live NHS implementation, primary stakeholder interviews or production-system testing were conducted as part of this portfolio exercise.

## Key Learning

The case demonstrates how requirements alignment, stakeholder engagement, process analysis, change management and governance contribute to planning complex enterprise technology transformations.

## Disclaimer

This is an independent portfolio adaptation of master's-level academic work. Recommendations are analytical proposals, not outcomes from an implemented solution.
